          What gets tested, how a paper trade is measured, how the verdict works, and what is still beta.

          How it works
          
        
            
          
          1. A strategy is one rule. "Buy coins that hit 50% bonding in under 5 minutes." The lab opened with 12 of the trenches' favorite rules.
            2. 
          
          2. The lab watches every new pump.fun launch. Around the clock, even when nobody has this page open. Young coins are read about every 15 seconds.
            3. 
          
          3. A match is a paper buy. The first time a coin meets a rule, the lab "buys" at the price at that moment. One trade per coin per strategy.
            4. 
          
          4. Three checkpoints. The price is read again 5, 15 and 60 minutes after entry.
            5. 
          
          5. The verdict. After 30 finished trades the Examiner rules. Losers go to the graveyard and the fleet proposes a replacement, so the board stays full.
            6. 
        
          
        
          A paper trade
          
        
          Entry is the coin's price the moment the lab sees it meet the rule. For threshold rules (like "crosses 90% bonding") the lab must have watched the coin cross the line, so no entry is ever back-dated.
          
        
          Result = exit price ÷ entry price, minus a flat 2% for fees and slippage. A trade "wins" if it is above 0% at the 60-minute checkpoint.
          
        
          Checkpoint sources. Most checkpoints are read live, within 60 seconds of their due time. If a checkpoint lands in a short gap between two lab runs, the lab reads the price for that exact minute from the coin's candle history instead. If neither is possible, the checkpoint is marked as a miss and the trade is left out of the stats. Every trade on a strategy page shows its source: live, candle, late or miss.
          
        
          Paper trades have no price impact and never get rugged mid-fill. Real fills are worse. Treat every number here as a best case.
          
        
          The verdict
          
        
          
          
          WhenTestResult

          A surviving strategy is judged again after every new trade. One bad stretch can still kill it, and it says so on its stone.

          FAQ
          
        
          Does the lab trade real money?
          No. Every trade is on paper. The lab never holds or moves funds.
          
        
          Why 60 minutes?
          Most pump.fun coins are decided in their first hour. 5 and 15 minutes show the path, 60 minutes is the verdict.
          
        
          Why the median and not the average?
          One 50x can carry a terrible strategy on average. The median is what a typical trade actually did.
          
        
          Can a refuted strategy come back?
          Not under the same rule. The fleet can propose a variant with different numbers, and that variant gets its own 30 trades.
          
        
          How fresh is the board?
          The lab publishes every few minutes. The lamp in the top bar shows when it last synced. The intake list and open trades also update live in your browser.
          
        
          Risks
          
        
          Surviving here means a rule did not lose on paper over its trades so far. It is not a promise about the next trade. Memecoins are extremely risky, most go to zero, and real fills are worse than paper. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

      how a trade is measured.
      
    
      The method in plain words: what gets tested, how a paper trade is scored, how the verdict works and what is still beta.
      
  
      
  
      
    
      the loop
      
    
        
      
      1. A strategy is one rule. "Buy coins that hit 50% bonding in under 5 minutes." The lab opened with 12 of the trenches' favorite rules.
        2. 
      
      2. The lab watches every new pump.fun launch, around the clock, even when nobody has this page open. Young coins are re-read about every 15 seconds.
        3. 
      
      3. A match is a paper buy. The first time a coin meets a rule, the lab buys at that moment's price. One trade per coin per strategy.
        4. 
      
      4. Three checkpoints. The price is read again 5, 15 and 60 minutes after entry.
        5. 
      
      5. The verdict. After 30 finished trades the Examiner rules. The dead go to the graveyard and their agent proposes a replacement.
        6. 
    
      
    
      a paper trade
      
    
      Entry is the price the moment the lab sees the coin meet the rule. For threshold rules the lab must have watched the coin cross the line, so no entry is ever back-dated.
      
    
      Result = exit price ÷ entry price, minus a flat 2% for fees and slippage. A trade wins if it is above 0% at the 60-minute checkpoint.
      
    
      Checkpoints are read live within 60 seconds of their due time. If one lands between two lab runs, the price for that exact minute comes from the coin's candle history, or it is read late (within 10 minutes). Otherwise it is a miss and the trade is left out of the stats.
      
    
      Paper trades have no price impact and never get rugged mid-fill. Real fills are worse. Treat every number here as a best case.

      the fine print.
      
    
      What's real, what's beta, and what it will never ask you for.
      
  
      
  
      
    
      is this financial advice?
      No. The lab shows how rules did on paper. A surviving rule only means it didn't lose over its trades so far. It is not a promise about the next one. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero.
      
    
      does the lab trade real money?
      No. Every trade is on paper. The lab never holds or moves funds.
      
    
      why 60 minutes?
      Most pump.fun coins are decided in their first hour. 5 and 15 minutes show the path, 60 minutes is the verdict.
      
    
      why the median and not the average?
      One 50x can carry a terrible strategy on average. The median is what a typical trade actually did.
      
    
      can a refuted strategy come back?
      Not under the same rule. An agent can propose a variant with different numbers, and that variant gets its own 30 trades.
      
    
      will refuted DM me?
      Never. Nobody from the lab will DM you, ask for your wallet or your seed phrase. Anyone who does is not us.
